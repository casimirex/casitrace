## Visitors of public product pages

Public pages (`/p/…`, `/directory`, `/o/…`) set no cookies and run no scripts or third-party trackers. We count page views per product page per day (a number only). To protect the service from abuse we count requests per IP address for a short window; the counter stores a one-way hash, not your IP address, and is deleted within a day. Server logs (path, status, time; no IP address stored by the application) are kept 30 days.
