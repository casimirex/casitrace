# ADR 0001: Pilot commodity and chain (D1)

- **Status:** Open — owner decision
- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Roadmap ref:** roadmap-v2.md §2

## Context

The KDE/CTE set, product model and capture screens depend on the commodity. Nothing in P1 depends on it, but P3 and P5 do.

## Decision

To be decided by the product owner before P3 starts. Candidate shape: one commodity, 3–5 producers → 1 processor → 1 distributor or retailer. Record the chosen commodity, partners and LOI status here.

## Consequences

P3 product fields and P5 KDE schemas stay generic until this is decided.
